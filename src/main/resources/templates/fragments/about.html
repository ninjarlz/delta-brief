📰 What is DeltaBrief?

DeltaBrief tracks long-running news topics — wars, politics, economics, regulations — and answers a different question than any feed or AI summarizer: not what's newest, but what changed. Every briefing compares a topic's current state against your last one, so you never have to scroll for 20 minutes to reconstruct what actually shifted. And unlike a feed you have to remember to check, it arrives as an email — a delta newsletter for the topics you're watching.
